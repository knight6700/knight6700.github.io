Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

In short: EngineerFit has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We never collect, sell or share your data.

What the app uses, and why

  • Apple Health (only if you allow it):
    • Reads your steps and walking distance to show your daily step goal and charts.
    • On Apple Watch, reads heart rate, active calories, steps and distance during workouts and walks you start.
    • Saves the workouts and walks you finish, if you turn on “Save workouts & walks to Apple Health”.
    • May ask for access to your health records to tailor your workout program. You can decline; the app works fully without it.
  • Motion & Fitness: counts your steps live during a walk on iPhone.
  • Apple Music (only if you turn it on): lists your library playlists so you can pick one, and plays, pauses and skips it during workouts and walks.
  • Notifications (only if you turn on reminders): a daily workout reminder, scheduled on your device.
  • Apple Watch: your iPhone and Watch exchange today’s plan, step totals and finished workouts and walks directly with each other through Apple’s Watch Connectivity.

Where your data is stored

Your program progress, streak, walk history and settings are stored only on your device. Data you allow the app to save to Apple Health is stored by Apple Health under your control. EngineerFit does not send any of your data to us or to anyone else.

Health data

Health data is used only to provide the app’s features on your device. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties. You can change or remove EngineerFit’s access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices (on Apple Watch: Settings › Health › Apps).

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Reset all progress and walks.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
  • Workouts and walks already saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Children

EngineerFit doesn’t knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
