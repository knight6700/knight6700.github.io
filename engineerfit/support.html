Common questions

My steps show 0 or “—”

Allow EngineerFit to read steps: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › EngineerFit, then turn on Steps and Walking + Running Distance. Steps from your iPhone and Apple Watch are combined by Apple Health.

How do I install the Apple Watch app?

On your iPhone, open the Watch app › My Watch › Available Apps, and tap Install next to EngineerFit. Open EngineerFit on your iPhone once so your Watch receives today’s workout.

The Watch says it needs Apple Health

Workouts and walks on Apple Watch run as Health workout sessions so they keep running with your wrist down. On your Watch, open Settings › Health › Apps › EngineerFit and allow access, then try again.

My playlist doesn’t start

Apple Music support needs Apple Music access (Settings › Privacy & Security › Media & Apple Music) and a playlist in your library. Songs that aren’t downloaded or purchased may need an Apple Music subscription. If other audio is already playing, EngineerFit leaves it alone.

How do I start over?

In the app, go to Settings › Reset all progress and walks. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there.

Is the workout safe for me?

EngineerFit is general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting if you have a health condition, and stop any exercise that causes pain.
